### Publications coming soon

#### Future of SP - Evolved Connectivity (May 2022): coined the term cloudlike-consumption, pre-dated the SP NaaS movement by a couple years

https://blogs.cisco.com/sp/perspectives-on-the-future-of-service-provider-networking-evolved-connectivity

Executive summary: Co-authored with Vaughn Suazo, the post argues that service providers must treat the network as a cloud-like resource pool for on-demand connectivity by standardizing end-to-end on SRv6/IPv6 (the Unified Forwarding Paradigm), reducing cross-domain complexity, and enabling SDN-driven, API-consumed “rich connectivity” services—from reachability to programmable, SLA-aware transport that applications and overlays can subscribe to on demand.

#### Future of SP - ML and AI (May 2022): focus on simplifying the infrastructure
https://blogs.cisco.com/sp/perspectives-on-the-future-of-service-provider-networking-the-role-of-machine-learning-and-artificial-intelligence

Executive summary: The post argues that service provider networks can catch up to cloud-native innovation by simplifying and standardizing end-to-end infrastructure (notably SRv6), collecting rich telemetry at scale, and automating operations first—creating the foundation for ML/AI-driven networks that self-heal, self-optimize, and enable demand-driven, customer-visible transport services rather than opaque workarounds.

#### Building an SRv6 uSID Data Center Fabric with SONiC (June 2023)
https://www.segment-routing.net/blogs/srv6-usid-on-sonic/

Executive summary: McDougall documents a hands-on proof-of-concept showing that SONiC’s Linux-native, containerized architecture pairs naturally with SRv6 uSID to deliver a simplified, BGP-unnumbered multi-tenant data center fabric—unifying segmentation, traffic engineering, and service chaining in one IPv6 forwarding plane while eliminating gateway stitching—with configs and lab artifacts published in the public srv6-labs/sonic-vs repository.
