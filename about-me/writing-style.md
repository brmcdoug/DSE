#### Sample letter of recommendation I wrote on behalf of Bob Gisiger

To Whom It May Concern:
 
I am very pleased to recommend Bob Gisiger for the FY24 Global Sales Advisory Board. I’ve known and worked closely with Bob for several years as he’s gone from very successful stints as an SE on the Apple team to his current senior architect/CTO position with the ASP Web Platforms group covering Apple, Equinix, Netflix, Oracle, Rackspace, Salesforce, and Twitter. Bob’s technical acumen and embrace of leading-edge technology make him a valuable advisor to this sophisticated set of hyperscale customers.

Bob has a breadth of experience covering all facets of hyperscale production networks (DC, WAN, and Optical), and has a talent for identifying trends and patterns, and collaborating across teams. A great example of this is the recent work he’s been leading with Web account teams and MIG Engineering to capture and document Cloud “Direct Connect” architectures and trends across AWS, Azure, Google, and Oracle Cloud, and how they might impact the future of the Service Provider industry as a whole.

I believe Bob’s experience in the Web/hyperscale market and his collaborative nature will make him a terrific asset to the GSAB team.
 
Please let me know if you have any questions.

Thanks,

Bruce McDougall
Principal Architect
Americas SP
