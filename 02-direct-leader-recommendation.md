Brook Crossman recommends Bruce McDougall for promotion to **Distinguished Systems Engineer**.

Across six Talent Assessment cycles (May 2023–January 2026), Brook has consistently rated Bruce as **meeting expectations** on both **Business Outcomes** and **Guiding Principles**. His manager’s narrative describes Bruce as a **senior technical leader in ASP** who combines strategic positioning for Cisco’s future with pragmatic, incremental customer delivery—understanding that **revenue comes first** while ensuring Cisco and its people are set up for long-term success.

**Performance and results.** Bruce’s impact spans patents and Bold Bets, Cisco Live speaking and lab ownership, customer proof-of-concepts (ATT, Megaport, Equinix, Twitch, Google, tier-1 hyperscalers and SPs), and internal/external thought leadership (SP360 blogs, SONiC lab, segmentrouting GitHub). Brook notes that Bruce is **one of, if not the leader in Segment Routing on the direct field team** and **represents Cisco incredibly well** in front of customers inside and outside ASP. In 1HFY26 Brook wrote that Bruce leads **frontier projects in AI forwarding**, that his innovation is **on pace to break ASP records** for patents filed and awarded, and that the **Pinnacle Award** recognition speaks to both technical depth and teamwork—*“Cisco is lucky to have him teaching our teams and customers, representing us externally and driving innovation in the portfolio.”*

**Collaboration and span.** Brook repeatedly cites volume of requests for Bruce’s help **outside ASP**, engineering forums’ trust in him as a representative, and feedback that **“everybody wants to work with him.”** Teams from **EMEA and APJ** seek his time for thought leadership. Bruce uses his reputation to advance account teams at Oracle, Google, Equinix, Bell, and others by bringing long-term innovation with pragmatic steps.

**People leadership.** Brook highlighted Bruce’s coaching of next-generation talent—including mentoring **Masiuddin Mohammed** to PSE—and, in later cycles, formal **PSE mentorship** (Nacho), participation in **Marina Ferreira’s** and Masi’s successful candidacies, and Google team interview support. Development discussions aligned on **adopting longer-term mentees** as a step toward DSE and on **delegating project requests** to others as visibility opportunities where appropriate.

**Progressive development (transparent).** Brook and Bruce agreed Bruce should **invest in executive communications**—more live SE engagements, upleveled messaging for senior audiences, and coaching on executive-ready narratives (2HFY25–1HFY26). Bruce self-identified needing a better **filter on inbound requests** (saying “yes” too often); Brook offered to help prioritize and, where possible, **route work to others for their development**. Earlier cycles included **AI technology learning** and identifying a **foundational DSE-linked project** with measurable business results (2HFY24). Bruce formally selected **Vaughn Suazo** as DSE mentor (bi-weekly, deliverable-driven) in 2HFY25.

**Future role if promoted to DSE.** Brook and Bruce are aligned on near-term priorities that define Bruce’s DSE scope: **SONiC SRv6 on Cisco 8000 G200/P200** for Microsoft and Oracle with production trial/deployment target **2H FY26**; **Microsoft** disaggregated T2 and disaggregated RH franchises; **Oracle** AI backend G200 SONiC trial; **Isovalent** SRv6 MVP from Beta to GA; **Comprehensive SRv6-for-AI demonstrator** with Marty, Josh, and Brent; repeatable **cloud-native segmentation architecture** (e.g., T-Mobile) with Vaughn; and continued **SE community multiplier** (Stay Ready Friday, labs, open source). A DSE Bruce would extend today’s ASP+Web AI/SRv6 center of gravity to **company-wide field and engineering alignment** on host networking, open NOS, and end-to-end SRv6—while growing the next generation of PSEs through sustained mentorship.
