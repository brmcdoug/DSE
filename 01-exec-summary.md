In 2012 with the early emergence of SDN and NFV, I realized the enormous potential in host-networking, of extending all of Cisco’s routing, switching, and network policy capabilities into the server where the application resides.

Our customers don’t think of themselves as “customers”, they think of themselves as network operators, or technology operators. This is an incredibly important distinction, because technology operators make decisions based on which technologies will allow them and their organization to be most successful. Business Outcomes.

Business Outcomes: the network exists to serve. Roads don’t have value unless they are used. Roads should be easy to use. For every network device running a network operating system, there are 10s, 100s, maybe 1000s of devices that run Linux, Windows, Android, iOS, etc. The Network has been a snowflake for a long time, but that is changing, and Cisco has an incredible opportunity to win that change.
